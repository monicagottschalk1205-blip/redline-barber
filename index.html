ABOUTATLANTA, GA

Est. 2019

Two brothers, two chairs, one rule: nobody leaves until the line is perfect.

Redline opened in 2019 on Edgewood Avenue in Atlanta's Old Fourth Ward, started by brothers Dre and Marcus Hale. It was two chairs and a borrowed mirror. Today it is five chairs, a full book and a line out the door on Saturdays, and the rule has not changed.

Every barber here trains in the shop before they ever take a client. Same blades, same standards, same hot towel.
